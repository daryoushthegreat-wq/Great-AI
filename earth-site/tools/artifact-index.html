Reading the Earth

How our planet was made, what lies beneath our feet, and why continents drift, mountains rise and volcanoes erupt. Fifteen short chapters, written for curious adults and updated with current science.

Start reading

Each chapter takes about five minutes to read. It has photographs from space, diagrams, a list of key terms and a few questions to check your understanding. Where popular older textbooks are now out of date, a Science today note gives the current view.
